04 — The thesis

Tourism still communicates like a brochure.

Seasonal campaigns, an annual media buy, a logo refresh every eight years, attribution nobody defends in a budget hearing. The category talks to travelers and reports to ministries in the same sentence.

The mandate has changed. A visitor economy is a portfolio: allocation, governance, conversion. The instruments belong to other industries — and those are the industries the winners will behave like.
