Layer 01 — Intelligence

Most tourism decisions are made on data that describes the past.

Arrivals, occupancy and spend are lagging indicators published one to two years after the fact. Intelligence is the discipline of measuring intent before it becomes economics.

02 — The lag

Intent is legible now. Arrivals are legible twenty months from now.

Both tracks describe the same demand. Only one of them exists at the moment the budget is set, which is why allocation is argued from the lower track and decided in the interval above it.
